# Claim Defensibility Matrix

Use for every high-visibility or high-intensity resume claim.

| Claim | JD relevance | Evidence class | Evidence strength | Ownership level | Validation maturity | Can explain? | Can modify/debug? | Real user validation? | Production validation? | Interview risk | Safe wording | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | High/Medium/Low | | Strong/Medium/Weak | L0–L5 | | Yes/Partial/No | Yes/Partial/No | Yes/No | Yes/No | Low/Medium/High | | Use / Downgrade / Omit / Build evidence |

## Selection rule

Prefer claims with:

> high JD relevance × high evidence strength × high ownership × low interview penetration risk

Do not use arithmetic to hide a hard boundary. A production claim without production evidence, or an independent-development claim below L4, remains blocked regardless of relevance.
