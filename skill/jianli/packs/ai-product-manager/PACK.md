# AI Product Manager Role Pack

## Scope

Use for AI Product Manager, Agent Product Manager, LLM application, enterprise AI, technical AI product, and AI-native workflow roles.

Do not automatically use for pure ML research, algorithm engineering, generic PM roles without AI product responsibility, or prompt-only content roles.

## Hiring thesis

A defensible AI PM claim should show that the candidate can:

1. identify a valuable user or business problem;
2. decide what belongs to Rule, Workflow, Agent, and Human;
3. translate business consequences into product and technical contracts;
4. build or coordinate a testable product with explicit personal ownership;
5. evaluate behavior, Bad Cases, failure handling, and validation maturity;
6. explain what remains unproven.

Vocabulary familiarity and AI-generated artifacts are weak substitutes.

## Priority problem classes

- complex business-system AI transformation;
- rule-dense or high-risk workflows;
- anomaly investigation and decision support;
- evidence, responsibility, and human confirmation;
- cross-system facts and state closure;
- Eval / Bad Case design;
- AI-assisted engineering ownership;
- real-user validation maturity.

## First-screen signals

Depending on the JD, prioritize:

1. user/business problem and product decision;
2. direct or adjacent workflow similarity;
3. personal decision and Capability Ownership Level;
4. Rule / Workflow / Agent / Human boundary;
5. validation stage and evidence tier;
6. technical mechanism as second-layer proof.

## Core gates

- Default AI Coding positioning: **AI-native product building and acceptance**.
- Use “independent full-stack development” only with L4+ engineering evidence.
- Demo/POC does not prove user value.
- Offline Eval does not prove real-user validation.
- Production context does not prove candidate L5 ownership.
- Missing project explanation right requires claim downgrade.
