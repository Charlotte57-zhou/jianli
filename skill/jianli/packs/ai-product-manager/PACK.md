# AI Product Manager Role Pack

## Scope

Use for:

- AI Product Manager;
- Agent Product Manager;
- LLM Application Product Manager;
- enterprise AI product;
- technical AI product;
- AI-native workflow product.

Do not automatically use for:

- pure ML research;
- algorithm engineer;
- generic software PM with no AI product requirement;
- prompt-only content roles.

## Hiring thesis

A strong AI PM resume should prove:

> the candidate can identify a valuable product problem, decide where AI should and should not be used, work with technical teams or AI coding tools to create a testable product, and evaluate whether the AI behavior is reliable enough for the intended workflow.

The resume should not merely prove familiarity with AI vocabulary.

## First-screen signals

Depending on the JD, prioritize:

1. product problem / user / business context;
2. AI product shipped, prototyped, or rigorously evaluated;
3. 0→1 or strong product decision evidence;
4. B2B / consumer / vertical-domain fit;
5. AI-native execution or technical collaboration.
