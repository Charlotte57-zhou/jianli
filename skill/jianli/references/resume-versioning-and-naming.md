# Resume Versioning and Naming

## Why naming matters

A tailored resume should be easy for:

- the candidate to track;
- recruiters to identify;
- interviewers to understand when forwarded internally.

## Recommended external filename

Use:

> 姓名-公司-岗位-简历.pdf

Examples:

> 张三-某科技公司-AI产品经理-简历.pdf
> 张三-某银行-企业AI产品经理-简历.pdf

For English applications:

> Firstname_Lastname_Company_Role_Resume.pdf

## Avoid

- 最终版.pdf
- 最终最终版2.pdf
- resume_new_new.pdf
- version numbers visible to recruiters unless needed
- internal notes in the filename
- overly long JD titles copied verbatim

## Internal version naming

Internally you may keep:

> master-v12
> company-role-2026-08-14-v2

But the exported file sent to the recruiter should be clean.

## Master resume naming

Use:

> 姓名-Master-Resume

or:

> 姓名-简历母版

The master should usually not be submitted directly unless it already matches the role.

## Maintain a version ledger

For each tailored version record:

- company;
- role;
- JD source/date;
- resume filename;
- major changes;
- claims added/removed;
- links shown/hidden;
- submission date;
- interview result;
- feedback.

This turns resume optimization into an iterative product process.
