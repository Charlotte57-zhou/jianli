# Project Hierarchy

Enterprise resumes often fail because organizational ownership is unclear.

## Four levels

### 1. Employer

The company that employed the candidate.

Example:

> Enterprise software vendor | Product Manager

### 2. Client / construction platform

The large customer-side program.

Example:

> National Grid Shared Finance Platform

### 3. Business modules / subprojects

Examples:

- distributed generation settlement;
- renewable subsidy calculation and settlement;
- extra-price fund accounting.

### 4. Candidate contribution

The actual work:

- business mapping;
- PRD/prototype;
- rule design;
- data mapping;
- interface definition;
- testing;
- on-site issue closure.

## Good structure

```text
WORK EXPERIENCE
Vendor Company | Product Manager

PROJECT EXPERIENCE
National Grid Shared Finance Platform
  Distributed Generation Settlement
  Renewable Subsidy Calculation & Settlement
  Extra-price Fund Accounting

Large public-utility enterprise | Finance-sharing customization projects
```

## Why grouping works

It avoids repeating:

> National Grid Shared Finance Platform | ...

three times in a row.

It also communicates that the candidate had **depth within a major platform**, not three unrelated mini-projects.

## Do not repeat role labels unnecessarily

If Work Experience already clearly states:

> Company | Product Manager

then every subproject does not need a separate “Role: Product Manager” line unless roles differ by project.
