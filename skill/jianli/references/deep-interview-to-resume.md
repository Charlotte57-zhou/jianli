# Deep Interview to Resume

## When to enter this mode

Use Deep Interview & Capability Discovery before resume writing when the candidate says they do not know their strengths, has fragmented experience, wants to explore talent, or is unsure which AI-assisted projects count as personal capability.

## Three-layer model

### 1. Natural Tendencies

Internal positioning hypotheses such as noticing inconsistencies, seeking structure, testing assumptions, or translating between perspectives.

Do not copy these labels directly into resume bullets.

### 2. Work-ready Capabilities

Capabilities supported by observable work or project behavior, evidence, and an ownership level.

### 3. Capability Gaps / Claim Boundaries

Capabilities the candidate wants but cannot yet defend, or claims whose evidence, ownership, explanation right, or validation maturity is insufficient.

## Interview method

Ask one core question at a time. Follow the weakest evidence point instead of completing a personality questionnaire.

For each candidate pattern collect:

1. a concrete episode;
2. trigger and constraints;
3. the candidate's observable action;
4. artifact or outcome;
5. another independent episode;
6. counterexample or failure;
7. external feedback if available;
8. energy/effort pattern as supporting context only.

Treat a repeated behavior as stable only when at least two episodes support it and no decisive counterevidence invalidates it.

## Conversion chain

```text
Natural tendency
→ Observable behavior
→ Capability primitive
→ Concrete project evidence
→ Ownership level
→ Validation maturity
→ Positioning or gap decision
```

Example:

```text
“容易发现哪里不对”
→ compares states, sources, and expected invariants
→ anomaly_inconsistency_detection
→ actual defect / reconciliation / Bad Case evidence
→ L2/L3 according to explanation and debugging evidence
```

Do not convert curiosity, learning ability, responsibility, or communication into high-value bullets without this chain.

## Exit gate

Before writing a resume, produce:

- stable behavior patterns and supporting episodes;
- work-ready capability primitives;
- ownership level and validation maturity for each;
- capability gaps and unsafe claims;
- strongest evidence-backed positioning;
- next evidence-building actions.
