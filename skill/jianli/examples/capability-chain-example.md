# Capability Chain Example

This is a synthetic local fixture. It demonstrates the decision chain, not candidate history or market frequency.

## Raw statement

> I used an AI coding tool to build an enterprise exception-handling Agent demo. I am good at finding problems and can independently develop AI products.

## Deep-interview episodes

### Episode 1

- Observed that rule results, source-system facts, and case status could disagree.
- Mapped the sources, states, responsibility owner, and recheck condition.
- Produced an exception path and acceptance cases.

### Episode 2

- A generated feature returned a stale answer after a new request.
- Traced request ownership, modified the cancellation condition, and ran focused regression cases.

## Stable behavior decision

- Natural tendency hypothesis: notices inconsistencies.
- Observable behavior: compares expected invariants, source facts, state, owner, and closure.
- Counterboundary: two episodes support the pattern; no real-user evidence is available.

## Capability primitives

- `anomaly_inconsistency_detection`
- `relation_boundary_state_modeling`
- `closure_validation`
- `evidence_based_iteration`
- `ai_native_product_building`

## Evidence and ownership

- Evidence Class: Verified by runnable demo, change record, and regression cases.
- AI contribution: generated substantial implementation code.
- Candidate evidence: can explain the design and call chain, modify key behavior, and reconstruct one debugging episode.
- Reproduction: full independent reproduction without prepared material has not been observed.
- Ownership Level: **L3 Modify / Debug**.
- Validation Maturity: **Offline Eval**.

## Career Positioning Audit

- Strongest positioning: AI Product Manager for complex enterprise workflows.
- Adjacent positioning: enterprise workflow / exception product roles.
- Not recommended: independent full-stack engineer.
- Largest gap: structured user review or pilot evidence; L4 reproduction evidence.

## JD mapping

Target requirement: design reliable Agent workflows and work with engineering to deliver testable products.

- Similarity: Direct/Adjacent.
- Relevant primitives: workflow decomposition, anomaly detection, closure validation, AI-native product building.
- Boundary: no production or measured business outcome.

## Claim Defensibility

Unsafe:

> Independently developed and launched a production-grade enterprise Agent platform.

Safe:

> 借助 AI Coding 构建企业异常处理 Agent Demo，负责规则/Agent/人工边界、状态闭环与验收设计；可解释核心调用链，修改关键行为并基于离线用例完成 Bad Case 回归。

## Interview evidence

The candidate should be able to explain:

- why stable cases remain deterministic;
- why the Agent is limited to ambiguous investigation;
- the core data and request flow;
- the stale-result Bad Case and debugging evidence;
- what Offline Eval proves;
- why the claim stops before real-user or production value.
