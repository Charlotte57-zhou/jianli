# Before / After Snippets

These are genericized examples.

## 1. Overclaiming ownership

### Before

> Led the 26-region distributed settlement system from 0→1.

### After

> Deeply participated in end-to-end distributed settlement product design and business mapping across 26 operating regions; independently led the paperless document-generation capability from 0→1.

Why: the system itself was mature, but the paperless capability was genuinely owned.

---

## 2. Public metric mistaken for internal impact

### Before

> Supported 120B RMB annual settlement.

### After

> Business coverage spanned 26 provincial operating regions, processing ~3M business-invoice relationships per month.

Why: the 120B figure was public context, not verified module throughput.

---

## 3. Estimated savings

### Before

> Saved 60M sheets of paper annually.

### After

> Paperless generation and electronic archiving covered at least 6M high-frequency business documents per month.

Why: document volume is verified; paper savings were estimated.

# Generic Tailoring Example

## Master evidence

> Coordinated requirements, data mappings, testing and rollout for a multi-system enterprise workflow.

## Tailored for Product Manager

> Defined business workflows, requirements and acceptance criteria across multiple enterprise systems, coordinating development and testing through rollout.

## Tailored for Business Analyst

> Modeled cross-system business processes and data mappings, clarified business rules and exception paths, and supported testing and rollout.

## Tailored for Project / Delivery Manager

> Coordinated cross-system requirements, development/testing dependencies and rollout issues across multiple stakeholder teams.

The facts are the same. The evidence emphasis changes.
